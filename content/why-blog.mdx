title: "Why blog?"
publishedAt: "0-0-0"
summary: "Why do I have a blog page."
---

Since coming to college, I have been incredibly busy. A mix of school work, club activities, recruiting, side projects,
exercise, etc. All that means that my day is filled.

I'm generally someone who loves to stay busy. I love
always having something to do and a goal to work towards. Yet this means that I often forget to look back on my progress,
and remember the highlights (and lowlights) of my journey. And I've noticed in the past that days pass by quick when I'm busy. 
The opportunity for reflection often fades away before I notice.

If you're a recruiter wanting to read about my accomplishments, I suggest checking my LinkedIn posts first. There,
I focus on writing about my achievements, focusing on details that would be helpful you. Of course, if you have any
questions, please feel free to contact me.

But, my blog is meant mostly for myself. With the advancements of AI, I find myself authentically writing much less.

I quote David McCullough, "Writing is thinking. To write well is to think clearly."

Reflecting on my journey, slowing down to think and write clearly, that's why I created this blog. It's public to hold myself
to consistent, clear writing, but it's created for my own reflection.
